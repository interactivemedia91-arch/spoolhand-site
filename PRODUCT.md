# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS generated by a small Python script (`tools/build.py` → `_site/`), plain JavaScript calculators (`static/js/fit.js`), GitHub Actions deploying to GitHub Pages on `spoolhand.app`. Confirmed by the owner on 19 Nov 2026, same approach as the Billdar site.

## Users

Industrial pipefitters, steamfitters and pipe welders, and their apprentices, mostly in the US. They search Google from a phone on site or at home for a quick answer: "rolling offset calculator", "pipe offset calculator", "elbow takeout chart". Often gloved, outdoors, on mobile data. Secondary: foremen and piping designers checking a dimension.

## Product Purpose

The free website of Spoolhand, a pay-once iPhone and iPad calculator and reference for pipefitters ($19.99, no subscription, works offline). The site gives away two calculators (simple/45° offset and rolling offset) and a butt-weld elbow takeout chart, with the same numbers and sources as the app, and leads visitors to the App Store. It also hosts the app's privacy policy and support page. Success: visitors who find a calculator through search get a correct answer fast, and some of them buy the app.

## Positioning

Every reference dimension comes from at least two independent published catalogs and shows where it came from; calculated values show their steps. The site's calculators are the app's own math, checked against the app's golden vectors for those two calculators (415 cases) and the handheld calculator guide's published worked examples. One purchase replaces a subscription calculator and a separate reference app. Competing 2026 apps also pitch "pay once, offline"; Spoolhand's difference is showing where each value comes from and how the math is checked.

## Operating Context

- The trade's language: offset, travel, run, set, roll, true offset, takeout, cut length, center to center, LR/SR elbows, weld gap, fractions to 1/16".
- Results read in inch fractions to 1/16" (e.g. `26-3/16"`), with millimeters available.
- Every reference page carries the disclaimer "Verify critical dimensions against the current standard before fabrication."
- The App Store listing links here for privacy (`/privacy/`) and support (`/support/`); support email `hello@spoolhand.app`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: home, `/pipe-offset-calculator/`, `/rolling-offset-calculator/`, `/elbow-takeout-chart/`, `/privacy/`, `/support/`, plus `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.
- No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts or fonts from other hosts. The App Store button carries an App Store campaign link (`pt`/`ct`), so App Store Connect counts visitors from the site without anything running on it. GitHub Pages keeps its own server logs; the privacy page says so.
- No trademarked product or competitor names in copy (Weldolet, Sockolet, Thredolet, Victaulic, Pipe Trades Pro, Blue Book, Calculated Industries, PipeSnap, FitterMaster, PipeBase). This is an editorial rule; naming a catalog's publisher as a source is attribution and stays.
- Data comes only from the app's `reference-data.json` (synced into `data/`); the calculators must pass the app's golden vectors.
- The App Store button reads "Coming soon" until the app is live; then it links to the App Store page.
- Undecided: the App Store URL (known at release).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Spoolhand; store name "Spoolhand: Pipefitter Calc".
- The app's "Blue Book" design system (`~/Code/pipefitter/design/design-system.md`) is binding for the site: warm paper, ink, safety yellow used only for the result and the current pipe; the app icon is the S-shaped offset of two elbows (`~/Code/pipefitter/design/icon/`).
- Voice: plain, the fitter's words, no hype; "pay once" and "no subscription" stated plainly.

## Product Principles

1. A correct number first: the answer is visible without scrolling once the inputs are in.
2. Show the source: every table value says where it came from.
3. The site is honest about the app: the same math, the price stated, nothing behind a sign-up.
4. Fast on a phone with gloves: big targets, no clutter, works on slow data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (the app's tokens meet 4.5:1 in the app; the site checks its own rendered pages), large touch targets, readable at arm's length, works without JavaScript for the chart and the static content.
